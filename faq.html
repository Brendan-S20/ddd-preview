General

What areas do you service?

All of Wayne County, Michigan. That includes Southgate, Taylor, Allen Park, Wyandotte, Trenton, Lincoln Park, Riverview, Woodhaven, Brownstown, Gibraltar, Flat Rock, Melvindale, Romulus, Dearborn, Dearborn Heights, Inkster, Garden City and the rest of Downriver.

How often can I schedule service?

One time, weekly, twice a week, bi-weekly or monthly. Commercial clients can set a custom schedule.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your property size, your number of dogs and how often we visit. No contracts. Just ask for a quote.

Do I need to be home?

Nope. As long as we can get to the area that needs cleaning, we'll scoop while you're out.

Are you insured?

Yep. We're fully insured for residential and commercial cleanup.

Do you sanitize the yard?

Not right now. We stick to what we do: picking up the poop.

Residential

What kinds of homes do you service?

Single-family homes, duplexes, rentals and other homes with a private or fenced yard.

Do you do one-time cleanups?

Sure do. Handy before parties, inspections and move-outs, or after the spring thaw.

Can I change or pause service?

Anytime. No contracts.

Where does the waste go?

We bag it and put it in your outdoor trash bin, unless you ask for something else.

Commercial

Do you service HOAs and apartment complexes?

Yep. Apartments, HOAs, condos, parks and pet-friendly businesses across Wayne County.

Can you handle more than one property?

Yes. We can build one custom schedule that covers them all.

Can we get a record of each visit?

Yes. We can send a digital update after each visit, for your files or tenant questions.
