General

What areas do you serve?

Downriver and Wayne County, Michigan. See the full list on the Service Areas page.

How often can I schedule service?

One time, weekly, every other week, twice a week or monthly. Commercial clients can set a custom schedule.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many dogs you have, the size of the yard or property and how often we visit. Ask for a free quote.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can get to the area that needs cleaning, we'll scoop while you're out.

Do you work in winter?

Yes. Service runs year-round.

Are you insured?

Ask Devin when you request a quote. The current website says the business is insured for residential and commercial work.†

Residential

What kinds of homes do you serve?

Single-family homes, duplexes, rentals and other homes with a private or fenced yard.

Do you do one-time cleanups?

Yes. They're handy before parties, inspections and move-outs, or after the spring thaw.

Can I change or pause service?

Yes. The current terms ask for a day's notice to cancel or reschedule a visit.†

Where does the waste go?

With us.† We bag it and haul it away.

Do you sanitize the yard?

Not right now. We stick to the poop.

Commercial

Do you serve HOAs and apartment complexes?

Yes. Apartments, HOAs, condos, parks, dog daycares and pet-friendly businesses across Wayne County.

Can you handle more than one property?

Yes. That's what the Custom Route is for.

Can we get a record of each visit?

Yes. We can send a digital update after each visit.†

Ready to get your yard back?

Tell us about your dogs and your yard. Devin sends your price and your first visit day.
