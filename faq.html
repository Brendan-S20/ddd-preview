Getting started

What areas do you serve?

Downriver and Wayne County, Michigan.† The full list is on the Service Areas page. Don't see your town? Ask in the quote form.

How much does it cost?

It depends on how many dogs you have, the size of the yard or property and how often Devin comes. Ask for a free quote and he sends you a price.

What should I put in the quote form?

Your dogs, roughly how big the yard is, how often you'd like him, how to get through the gate, and anything he should watch for.

How often can I schedule service?

Twice a week, weekly, every other week or monthly, or The Reset for a single cleanup. Properties can set a custom route.

What kinds of homes do you serve?

Single-family homes, duplexes, rentals and other homes with a private or fenced yard.

Is the shelter coupon still offered?

No current offer. The News Herald reported in May 2025 that Taylor Animal Shelter and Lincoln Park Animal Shelter were putting coupons for a free yard cleaning in adoption packets. That was then. Your quote is free either way.

Visit day

Do I need to be home?

No.† Leave the gate unlocked and the dogs inside, and Devin scoops while you're out.

What happens on visit day?

Devin comes through the gate, walks the whole yard front to back, and bags the poop as he goes. Then it leaves with him.†

What if my gate is locked?

The current terms say he may have to skip the visit.† Leave it unlocked, or tell him how to get in.

Do I need to cut the grass?

It helps. He scoops what he can see, and long grass hides it.

Where does the waste go?

With us.† We bag it and haul it away.

Do you sanitize the yard?

Not right now. We stick to the poop.

Can I reschedule or cancel a visit?

Yes. Give Devin a day's notice. That's what the current terms ask for.†

Winter

Do you work in winter?

Yes.† Service runs year-round.

Why clean up before the snow?

Whatever's out there now gets buried and shows up again in spring. A Reset before the snow sticks means spring is just spring.

What about after the thaw?

That's a good time for The Reset too. Start a schedule after it, or don't.

Properties

Do you serve HOAs and apartment complexes?

Yes. Apartments, HOAs, condos, parks, dog daycares and pet-friendly businesses across Wayne County.†

Which areas can you cover?

Whatever you put on the list: common areas, sidewalks, courtyards, trails, dog runs and green spaces.

Which routes can a property choose?

The Steady Route (weekly), The Lighter Route (every other week) or The Custom Route.

Can you handle more than one property?

Yes. That's what The Custom Route is for.

Can we get a record of each visit?

Yes. We can send a digital update after each visit.†

Are you insured?

Ask Devin when you request a quote. The current website says the business is insured for residential and commercial work.†

About Devin

Who runs Detroit Doo Disposal?

Devin Rocz. He started it with a spring-break offer to clean yards, and it grew into a year-round business.

Is it really Devin who comes?

It's Devin's business, and he's still the guy with the scooper. UM-Dearborn News reported in November 2025 that he'd hired an employee as it grew.† Ask when you get your quote.

Where can I read about the business?

UM-Dearborn News and The News Herald both wrote about it. Links are on the In the news page.

Ready to get your yard back?

Tell us about your dogs and your yard. Devin sends your price and your first visit day.
