About Detroit Doo Disposal

A locally owned pet waste removal business, started by Devin Rocz and based Downriver.

How it started

In early 2025, Devin Rocz was cleaning his own yard as the snow melted and figured other people needed the same help. Over spring break he posted an offer to clean yards for pay on a Downriver community page. The response came within hours.

That offer became Detroit Doo Disposal, launched in February 2025. It grew into a year-round business cleaning residential yards, apartment complexes, public parks and doggy daycares.

Devin was a finance student at the University of Michigan-Dearborn at the time, and has said his business classes helped him run the company.
