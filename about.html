About Detroit Doo Disposal

Devin Rocz's pooper scooper business. It started with his own dogs and his own backyard.

How it started

Devin Rocz has dogs of his own: Oreo, Tucker and Axel. Cleaning up after them as the snow melted, he kept finding surprises, and he figured the neighbors had the same problem.

He launched Detroit Doo Disposal in February with the slogan “They poop, we scoop.” Over UM-Dearborn's spring break, he posted an offer to clean yards on a Downriver community page. The response came within hours.

He was a finance major in UM-Dearborn's College of Business at the time, after transferring from Henry Ford College.

Sources: UM-Dearborn News (November 5, 2025) and The News Herald (May 19, 2025). See the stories →

Where he learned it

Devin's dad started Father and Son Restoration in Taylor. Devin says that's where he learned hard work, kindness and what relationships in a community are worth. It shows up in small ways, like doing the whole yard, not just the middle of it.

Source: UM-Dearborn News, November 5, 2025.

What it is now

Today Detroit Doo Disposal cleans residential yards, public parks, apartment complexes and doggy day cares, and it works year round. Devin built a website, set up a customer system and hired an employee. He's still the guy with the scooper.

Source: UM-Dearborn News, November 5, 2025.
