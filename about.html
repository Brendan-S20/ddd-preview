Meet Devin, the guy with the shovel

Devin founded Detroit Doo Disposal with a strong work ethic, a soft spot for animals and deep roots in Downriver, Michigan.

He saw that Downriver needed a dog poop cleanup service you could actually count on, so he started one. He keeps it hands-on and consistent, and he cares about the details.

Homes, HOAs, parks and property managers get the same thing every visit: a clean yard and a neighbor who takes pride in the work.
