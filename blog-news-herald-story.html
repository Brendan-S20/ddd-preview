The News Herald on a spring-break job that became a business

A summary of The News Herald's May 2025 story.

The News Herald published Susan Thwing's story, "Spring break hustle becomes Detroit Doo Disposal," on May 19, 2025. It described Devin, a UM-Dearborn finance major, looking for work over spring break.

As the paper told it, Devin had just cleaned his own yard and figured he could do the same for others. He posted a flyer on a Downriver community page, and the response came within hours. The May 19, 2025 story quoted him: "It just absolutely blew up."

The paper also reported that Devin had recently made the business an LLC.

The same story reported partnerships with Taylor Animal Shelter and Lincoln Park Animal Shelter, which put coupons for a free yard cleaning in adoption packets. That describes May 2025. It isn't a current offer.
