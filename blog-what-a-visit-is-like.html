What a cleanup visit is like

From the quote form to the gate to where the poop goes. Here's what happens when Devin comes to your yard, and what you don't have to do.

First, the quote form

Tell Devin about your dogs, roughly how big the yard is, how often you'd like him, how to get through the gate, and anything he should watch for. Rather talk? Call (734) 301-7232.

Your price comes down to three things: how many dogs, how big the yard, and how often he comes. Devin sends the price and your first visit day.

Pick how often he comes

  • The Full Pack, twice a week, for busy yards and multi-dog homes.
  • The Regular, weekly. Most dogs, most yards.
  • The Easygoing, every other week, for one dog or a quiet yard.
  • Light Duty, monthly, for yards that stay pretty tidy.
  • The Reset, one big cleanup. Start a schedule after it, or don't.

Not sure? Tell him about the dogs and he'll point you to one. Need to reschedule or cancel a visit? Give Devin a day's notice. That's what the current terms ask for.†

Before he shows up

Unlock the gate. If he can't get in, the current terms say he may have to skip the visit.† Keep the dogs inside while he's in the yard. It's quicker for him and calmer for them. And keep the grass cut: he scoops what he can see, and long grass hides it.

On visit day

You don't need to be home.† Devin comes through the gate, walks the whole yard front to back, corners the dogs favor included, and bags the poop as he goes.†

Where the poop goes

With him. It's bagged up and hauled away, not left in your trash can.† (The current detroitdoodisposal.com still says waste is bagged and left in your bin.)

What he doesn't do

No yard sanitizing right now. We stick to the poop. And snow doesn't cancel it: service runs year-round, through snow, sun and spring mud.†

What you don't have to do

Be home. Find the bag. Make room in the trash can. Step in it on the way to the grill.

They poop, we scoop. Let Devin handle the dirty work.
