What's a visit like? Here's the scoop

From booking to bagging, here's how a home cleanup works. Spoiler: you don't have to be there.

Start by booking online or by phone at +1 (734) 301-7232. Next, your quote gets confirmed and your first visit day gets set. The quote depends on your dogs, your yard size and how often we visit.

Pick the rhythm that fits: weekly, every other week or just one time. Twice-a-week and monthly service are options, too. There are no contracts, and you can pause or change your service anytime.

On visit day, you don't need to be home, as long as we can get to the area that needs cleaning. We scoop, bag it and put it in your outdoor trash bin, unless you ask for something else.

We stick to picking up poop, so we don't offer yard sanitizing right now. We do scoop year-round, through snow, sun and spring mud.
