UM-Dearborn on how Detroit Doo Disposal started

A summary of UM-Dearborn's November 2025 story about Devin Rocz and the business.

On November 5, 2025, UM-Dearborn published "Building a business, one scoop at a time," a story by Sarah Tuxbury. It reported that Devin Rocz, then a College of Business junior majoring in finance, launched Detroit Doo Disposal in February 2025. The slogan: “They poop, we scoop.”

According to the story, the idea came while Devin was cleaning his own yard as the snow melted. As something to do over spring break, he posted on social media offering yard cleanups for pay. The post got a big response.

UM-Dearborn reported that it grew into a year-round business cleaning residential yards, apartment complexes, public parks and doggy day cares. Along the way, Devin launched a website and set up a customer management system.

The story also said his business classes help, including QuickBooks in an information systems course and a project management course for planning growth.

The same UM-Dearborn story from November 5, 2025 shared Devin's advice: "Think about something that would make your life easier and see if there’s a demand."
