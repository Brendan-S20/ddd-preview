This part's just the quote

The form asks for a price. Nothing's on the calendar until Devin sends your price and your first visit day and you're good with both.† The more he knows about the yard now, the closer that price is to the real thing.

What to put in it

  • Your dogs. How many, and whether any of them likes to slip out a door.
  • Your yard. Roughly how big, front or back or both, and whether it's fenced.
  • How often. Pick a schedule, or write “not sure” and Devin will point you to one.
  • How to get in. Side gate, back gate, a latch that sticks. If he can't get in, the current terms say he may have to skip the visit.†
  • Anything to watch for. A garden bed, a loose fence board, a spot the dogs dug up.

How the first visit starts

On your first day, Devin comes in through the gate you left unlocked.† The dogs stay inside. He starts with the spots they use most, then works the rest of the yard front to back so nothing's hiding in a corner. Everything goes in a bag, and the bag leaves with him.† Then the yard's yours again. Let the dogs back out.
