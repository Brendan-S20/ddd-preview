Here's what a visit looks like

No property names, no stock photos. Just what Devin does on the day.

  1. He shows up on your day†

    On the route you set. Nobody has to meet him at the gate.

  2. He walks every area on your list

    The dog run, the courtyard, the grass strip by the sidewalk, the edge of the trail. If it's on the list, he walks it.

  3. It leaves with him†

    Bagged as he goes and hauled away.†

  4. Winter included†

    Snow on the ground doesn't cancel the walk.

Property manager questions

Which routes can a property choose?

The Steady Route (weekly), the Lighter Route (every other week) or a Custom Route.

Can one route cover more than one property?

Yes. That's what the Custom Route is for.

Can we get a record of each visit?

Yes. Devin can send a digital update after each visit.†

Are you insured?

Ask Devin when you request a quote. The current website says the business is insured for residential and commercial work.†

Tell Devin about the property

Send these and the quote fits the property.

  • What kind of property. Apartments, an HOA, a condo, a park, a daycare or a business.
  • Which areas. The dog run, the courtyard, the strips along the sidewalk. A rough site map helps.
  • How big, roughly. Square footage, or how many separate areas he'll cover. That's what the price is built on.†
  • How often. The Steady Route, the Lighter Route or the Custom Route.
  • More than one site? Say so. One route can cover them.
