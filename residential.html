Before Devin's first visit

Four small things, so the first visit goes right.

  • Unlock the gate. If he can't get in, the current terms say he may have to skip the visit. Leave it unlocked, or tell him how to get in.
  • Keep the dogs inside. Just while he's in the yard. It's quicker for him and calmer for them.
  • Tell him what to watch for. A garden bed, a loose fence board, a dog that slips out the side door. Put it in the quote form.
  • Keep the grass cut. He can scoop what he can see. Long grass hides it.
