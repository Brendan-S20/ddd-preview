About Devin

A UM-Dearborn story

Devin Rocz was a junior in UM-Dearborn's College of Business, majoring in finance, when he started Detroit Doo Disposal. He'd transferred in from Henry Ford College. The idea came while he was cleaning up after his own dogs, and he posted an offer on social media to clean yards over UM-Dearborn's spring break. UM-Dearborn News told the story under the headline "Building a business, one scoop at a time."

Source: UM-Dearborn News, November 5, 2025.
